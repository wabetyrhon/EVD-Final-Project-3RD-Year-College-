About us

Laortealbe Meat Shop is a neighborhood frozen meat shop in Cebu City. We sell pork, chicken, fish, hotdogs and other frozen food, so households, workers and students nearby can buy good meat without traveling to a big market.

Buy by the kilo or by the pack, whichever fits your meal and your budget. Our promise is simple: fresh, properly stored meat at reasonable prices, and service from people who know their neighbors.
